Cut to the Kevin × Fable beat sheet: dual-mode heartbeat, match-cuts, a revealing lens, scale jumps, chaos → grid, breathe/strike. Gill's colour, texture and humour are the paint language, not the pacing.

01

A Shopping List, Mostly Kept

Seven things written down, snapped between paper and a scorched dark, thrown loose, ruled up as an inventory for half a second, and left as a constellation. One word at monument scale; the thing she crossed off catches fire.

14s loop · 8-cut strike at 8.4s

 02

Two Umbrellas Having Words

Two feet on a wet pavement that never move, and everything above them leaning further in until they collide. The canopies come apart into twelve torn panels and file themselves as a colour chart. A lens dragged across the afternoon has the same street at night inside it.

13.5s loop · 10-cut strike at 8.2s

 03

Showers Later, Mainly in the Cup

An iris opened at the mouth of the spout and grown until the whole kitchen is inside its own weather. The cloud comes apart into twelve slips and lines up as a forecast — a week of weather issued by a kettle — before it rains into the cup anyway.

13s loop · 10-cut strike at 8.0s
