# Beat Desk

A phone-first explain film. One idea is four beats on a sheet. Open `index.html` and Following distance is already performing. No build, no npm, no network, no accounts, no audio.

Double-click `tech-demos/beat-desk/index.html`, or from the repo root:

```
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/tech-demos/beat-desk/`.

Optional query string:

- `?pack=row` opens right-of-way. Also `follow` (default), `commute`, `custom`.
- `?t=6.5` seeks that many seconds into the 16-second film.
- `?pause=1` holds the sheet.

If the browser asks for less motion, the idle glint, rain drift, and heat wobble stay still. The four beats still play. Pause holds a frame.

## What John does with his hands

- Picks a pack: Following distance, Right-of-way, Commute feel, or Custom.
- Watches the sheet perform, then drags the thumb. The picture is a function of the thumb, so the count, the gap, and the clocks move backward as well as forward.
- Taps a beat to play that performance from its start. Play and pause sit in the bottom dock. Space does the same when a label is not focused.
- Drags the four beats into a new order (or Alt + arrow on a focused grip). The scrubber’s left-to-right order becomes that order. The thumb stays on the beat he was in.
- On Custom, rewrites the four labels. The words he types are the claim the sheet circles, stamps, counts, or holds.

## What he learns

The two driving packs are lessons for his driver-ed loop (TigerTest). The commute pack is a San Diego house-scout feel-sketch. Mile and minute figures on that sheet are stamped SKETCH.

Each beat is 4 seconds. Four beats, in the order on the desk, are the film.

### Following distance

The space is the lesson. Pick a mark the lead car passes and count one, two, three before you reach it. The same three seconds is a short stretch in town and a long one at speed: about 110 feet at 25 mph, about 286 feet at 65 mph (`mph × 1.4667 × 3`, rounded on the sheet). Rain, fog, or dark: count six, and the car settles farther back.

### Right-of-way

At a four-way stop, the first car to arrive is the first to go. If two arrive together, the driver on the left yields to the driver on the right. On the sheet that is the car at the bottom (you) yielding to the car on your right. A left turn waits while straight-on traffic clears. Right-of-way is given: the sheet shows a lunge into a gap that was not offered, then the car going once the gap is given.

### Vista and Oceanside

Same alarm, two doors. Vista is inland on the 78. Oceanside is the coast and the 5. Sketch miles finish the inland arc first, which is the picture a listing likes. Sketch minutes keep the coast clock running after the inland one stops (28 and 41 in the sketch). The weekday on offer is a shorter oven on the 78, or longer cooler air on the 5.

### Custom

The four moves still perform: an arc closes, a stamp lands, a count climbs, a sheen holds. The lesson is whatever he writes on the labels.
